# Word hack minigame

**Source:** `word_hack.c` (80K)

A standalone word-based hacking minigame. Uses `instructional_buttons` and
`GENERIC_INSTRUCTIONAL_BUTTONS`.

Small and self-contained, unlike the heist hacking which is buried inside 10 MB
mission controllers. This is the one to read if you want to understand how R*
structures a hacking minigame.
