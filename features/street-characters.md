# Street characters and strangers

**Source:** 14 `gpb_*` scripts, all exactly 68K (Pamela Drake 72K)

`gpb_andymoon`, `gpb_baygor`, `gpb_billbinder`, `gpb_clinton`, `gpb_griff`,
`gpb_jane`, `gpb_jerome`, `gpb_jesse`, `gpb_mani`, `gpb_mime`,
`gpb_pameladrake`, `gpb_superhero`, `gpb_tonya`, `gpb_zombie`.

One script per character, identical structure and size. No anim dicts — they run
on scenarios and ambient speech rather than bespoke animation.

Together with the four `pb_*` performers
(see [street-performers.md](street-performers.md)) that is 18 worked examples of
a wandering ambient character.
