`tennis_network_mp.c`, `launcher_tennis.c`

- Anim dicts `mini@tennis`, `mini@tennis@female`, `mini@tennisexit@male`,
  `mini@tennisexit@female`

  `facials@gen_female@variations@happy`, `facials@p_m_two@base`

The facial variation is what sells the rallies.
