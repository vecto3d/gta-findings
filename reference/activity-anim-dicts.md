animation sweep, because those prefixes are activity-specific by convention.

## Sweep that was low yield

Filtering anim dicts to those used in two scripts or fewer returns mostly
standard `amb@world_human_*` scenario clipsets — guard patrol, hang out street,
stand impatient, smoking, tourist mobile. Well-known animations, not hidden
mechanics. Use the `mini@`/`oddjobs@` prefix sweep instead.
